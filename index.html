About the studio

Design that starts with your needs.

Space Composers is a multidisciplinary firm of architects and interior design professionals based in Ludhiana. We work on homes and offices, and we begin every project by learning how the space will be used.

Layout, light, materials and the small details are decided together, so each room feels intentional and works well day to day.
